# Before a 1:1

Write the agenda the person reads before a 1:1. It is about **their work and what gets in its way**, never a judgement
of them. It is the person's own: it is never sent or shown to anyone.

## Read

1. Find them in Jira (their account) and in GitHub (their login) by the name given. Not found, or more than one: say
   so in the doubts, and read nothing more about anyone.
2. Jira: the issues they own or were on since the last 1:1 (else the last two weeks): done, in work, blocked.
3. GitHub: their pull requests in the repositories named, and the reviews they gave, over the same time.
4. No Slack: what someone writes in the channels is not an agenda item about them.

## Write the agenda (Markdown)

- **Their work since the last 1:1** — done, in work, reviews they gave; each with its link.
- **Where they got stuck** — the items, not them: waiting for a review, blocked, reopened; with links.
- **Last time** — what was said and promised at the last 1:1 (from *Last time*), and what came of it.
- **Your notes** — the person's notes, as they wrote them. Add nothing to them, change nothing.
- **To ask** — at most 5 open questions about their work, their aims and what gets in their way.

## The line

- Facts with links; questions, not conclusions.
- No strengths or weaknesses, no score, no ranking, no comparison with anyone, no reading of their tone or mood, no
  counts of commits, messages or hours as a measure of them.
- Invent no fact, no name, no date, no number: what you could not find goes into the doubts.
- Write in the language the person wrote in.
