# A Jira task

Write one Jira issue from what the person said needs doing.

## Find before you write

1. Find the Jira site (its cloud id) and the project: the one the person named, else the one they used last. If
   none fits, say so in the doubts.
2. If an epic is named (a key like `ABC-12` or words of its name), find it in that project. One match: use its key
   as the issue's parent. None, or more than one: say so in the doubts, and leave it out.
3. Look for an open issue in the project with a like summary from the last 90 days. If there is one, name it in the
   doubts with its key, its summary, its status and how old it is: it may be the same work.

## Write

- **Title** (`summary`): an action, as the person would say it to a colleague — *Fix the checkout on Safari*,
  *Add an export of the invoices*. At most 80 characters, no full stop.
- **Description**, in two short parts:
  - **Why** — what is wrong or wanted, and for whom, in the person's words.
  - **Done when** — how anyone can tell it is done: a check, a result, a number.
- Use only what the person said and what you found. Invent no fact, no name, no date, no number. What you do not
  know stays out, or goes into the doubts.
- Write in the language the person wrote in.
