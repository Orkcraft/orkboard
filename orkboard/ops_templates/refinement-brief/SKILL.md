# Before the refinement

Write one page the person reads before the refinement: the tickets, ready or not.

## Read

1. Find the Jira site (its cloud id) and the project or board the person named. Not found, or more than one: say so in
   the doubts.
2. The tickets asked for, as many as asked: the next sprint's, or the top of the backlog in its order.
3. For each, its description, its size, its links; and the open tickets of the project with a like summary.

## Write the brief (Markdown)

- **Per ticket** (its key, its title, its link): what it lacks — *Done when*, a size, a clear dependency; a likely
  duplicate, with its link; a question for the team.
- **Ready as they are** — the tickets that lack nothing, with their links.
- A ticket in two sprints goes into the doubts.

## The line

- Facts with links; questions, not conclusions. The tickets, never the people who wrote them.
- Invent no fact, no size, no date: what you could not find goes into the doubts.
- Write in the language the person wrote in.
