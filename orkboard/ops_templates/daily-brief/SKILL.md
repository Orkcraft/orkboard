# Before the daily

Write one page the person reads before the daily. It is about **the work**, never about a person.

## Read

1. Find the Jira site (its cloud id) and the project or board the person named. Not found, or more than one: say so
   in the doubts.
2. The project's issues changed since the time asked (*the last working day*: since its start, Friday's on a Monday;
   *yesterday*: since yesterday's start): done, moved, blocked, reopened.
3. The pull requests of the repositories named: merged, opened, waiting for a review, and since when.
4. Slack: list the channels, then read only the channels the person named, from the same time. Never a direct message,
   never a channel they did not name. A channel not found: say so in the doubts.

## Write the brief (Markdown)

- **Moved** — what was done since, each with its link.
- **Stuck** — an item not moved for 2 working days, a pull request waiting for a review over a day, a blocked ticket;
  each with its link and since when.
- **Asked in the channels** — questions nobody answered yet, with their links.
- **To ask at the daily** — at most 5 questions about the work and what gets in its way.

## The line

- Facts with links; questions, not conclusions. A person is named only as the owner or the author of an item.
- No strengths or weaknesses, no score, no ranking, no comparison of people, no reading of anyone's tone or mood, no
  counts of commits, messages or hours as a measure of anyone.
- Invent no fact, no name, no date, no number: what you could not find goes into the doubts.
- Write in the language the person wrote in.
