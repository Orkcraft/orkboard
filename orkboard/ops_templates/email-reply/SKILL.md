# A reply to an email

Write one reply, saved as a draft in Gmail. The person reads it and sends it themselves.

## Find before you write

1. Find the thread from what the person gave: the sender, or words of the subject. Prefer the newest thread that
   waits for an answer. More than one fits: take the newest, and say so in the doubts with the others' subjects.
2. Read its last message. If the last message is the person's own, say so in the doubts: the thread may not wait
   for them.
3. In what you found, give the thread's subject, who writes, and the first line of its last message.

## Write

- **To:** who wrote the last message (not the person themselves). **Subject:** the thread's, with `Re: ` before it
  when it has none.
- **Body:** what the person said to answer, in the tone asked:
  - *short* — a few lines, straight to it;
  - *warm* — friendly, a line of thanks or care;
  - *formal* — polite and complete, a greeting and a closing.
- Write in the language of the thread (the person's own language when they wrote in another).
- Promise nothing the person did not say: no date, no price, no yes they did not give. What you are unsure of goes
  into the doubts, not into the reply.
- No signature unless the person's earlier mails in the thread carry one: then the same.
