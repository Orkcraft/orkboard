Fantasy signs: the candidates

Each goal type's sign, drawn as a thing of the realm (spec 56 §2). The sign a type wears today is first, then its candidates, each at 28 and 14 px, in ink, in muted ink and in the five element colours. The library and the reserved signs follow.
